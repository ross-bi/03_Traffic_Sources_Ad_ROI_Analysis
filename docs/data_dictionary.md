## 3. Key Metrics Definitions

| Metric | Formula | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| CTR (Click-Through Rate) | `clicks ÷ impressions` | Percentage of impressions that result in a click |
| CVR (Conversion Rate) | `orders ÷ sessions` | Percentage of sessions that result in an order |
| Click CVR | `orders ÷ clicks` | Percentage of clicks that result in an order |
| CPC (Cost Per Click) | `spend ÷ clicks` | Average cost per click |
| CPM (Cost Per Mille) | `spend ÷ impressions × 1,000` | Cost per 1,000 impressions |
| CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) | `spend ÷ orders` | Cost per converted order |
| ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) | `revenue ÷ spend` | Revenue generated per dollar of ad spend (multiplier) |
| ROI% | `(revenue − spend) ÷ spend × 100` | Return on investment as a percentage |

| Facebook Ads | 2.2% | 2.5% | $88 | Awareness / Conversion / Retargeting |

| Organic | N/A | 3.0% | $85 | SEO |

**Campaign Type Modifiers (relative to baseline CVR):**

| Campaign Type | CVR Multiplier | CPC Multiplier | Notes |
|--------------|---------------|----------------|-------|
| Search | 1.20x | 1.8x | High active intent; CVR above average |
| Shopping | 1.10x | 0.9x | Product imagery drives stronger purchase intent |
| Display | 0.70x | 0.5x | Primarily brand awareness; lower CVR |
| Awareness | 0.60x | 0.8x | Pure top-of-funnel; lowest CVR |
| Conversion | 1.30x | 1.5x | Directly optimised for conversions; high efficiency |
| Retargeting | 1.50x | 1.2x | Re-engaged audiences with strong purchase intent |
| Newsletter | 1.00x | 0.1x | Baseline CVR for subscribers; very low cost |
| Promotion | 1.40x | 0.1x | Promotion-driven; high CVR at low cost |
| Automation | 1.60x | 0.05x | Abandoned cart trigger; highest CVR, lowest cost |
| SEO | 1.00x | 0.0x | No ad spend |
| Direct | 1.00x | 0.0x | No ad spend |

> All values are fixed with `random.seed(42)`. A seasonality multiplier is applied: Q4 × 1.3, Q1 spring × 1.1, all other periods × 1.0.
